## The Three Archetypes

Every great presentation needs three perspectives:

| Archetype | Focus | Key Question | Tools |
|-----------|-------|--------------|-------|
| **The Architect** | Logic & Structure | "Is this logically sound?" | MECE, Pyramid Principle, Deductive/Inductive |
| **The Storyteller** | Narrative & Emotion | "Does this resonate?" | Sparklines, Tension, Hero's Journey |
| **The Designer** | Visuals & Clarity | "Is this clear at a glance?" | Data-Ink Ratio, Gestalt, Visual Hierarchy |

## The 10-Step Process Summary

```
Phase 0: Foundation
└── Step 0: Launchpad (SCQA → Big Idea → Pillars)

Phase 1: Block Level (Strategic Architecture)
├── Step 1: Structure the Problem (Architect)
├── Step 2: Define the Journey (Storyteller)
└── Step 3: Plan the Evidence (Designer)

Phase 2: Loop Level (Argumentative Flow)
├── Step 4: Sequence the Argument (Architect)
├── Step 5: Manage the Tension (Storyteller)
└── Step 6: Storyboard the Data (Designer)

Phase 3: Slide Level (Execution & Polish)
├── Step 7: Sharpen the Insight (Architect)
├── Step 8: Visualize the Concept (Storyteller)
└── Step 9: Refine the Signal (Designer)
```
